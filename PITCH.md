# 3-minute pitch script

**0:00 Hook.** "ChatGPT and Gemini *talk*. Pragati talks **and acts** — and it knows your family. It's JARVIS, but desi."

**0:15 Open the app.** Start the demo. Show the ChatGPT-style layout: chats on the left, the living orb, suggestion cards.

**0:30 Ask anything.** Type `Who was APJ Abdul Kalam?`. The answer streams in with sources and a photo. Then `Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0`, which comes back with steps and proper maths formatting. With a Gemini key, it reads a homework photo: tap **+**, add a picture, and ask "solve this".

**1:00 Family.** Say `call Mummy`. Pragati doesn't know the number yet, so it asks inline. Save it, and it dials after a visible 3-second countdown you can cancel. Then `Papa ko WhatsApp karo ki main 8 baje tak aaunga` opens WhatsApp with the text ready, and `Didi ko birthday wish karo` writes the wish too.

**1:40 Apps.** `play Kesariya on YouTube`, `open Instagram`, `petrol pump near me`.

**1:50 Hands-free + every language.** Tap **Voice**. Say `Papa ko WhatsApp karo`; Pragati asks what to say, you speak it, and WhatsApp opens. Then ask something in Tamil or Spanish: Pragati detects the language and answers in it, with live captions.

**2:05 Personal advice.** `I feel stressed about exams, what should I do?` Pragati answers like a caring elder sibling and uses what it remembers ("your boards start 15 Feb…").

**2:20 Acts with control.** `Plan my study for 2 hours and remind me at 7 pm` shows a plan. Approve it, and the tasks and reminder are created, with undo. Show the Trust center: a tamper-evident ledger of every action.

**2:50 Close.** "Ask anything. Study everything. Get it done. Pragati — your AI, like family."

## Likely judge questions
- **Is the AI real?** Yes. With a free Gemini key it uses Google's Gemini with live Google Search grounding, and the top bar shows the active engine. With no key, an offline brain still answers from Wikipedia, does maths and runs every action.
- **Can it really make calls and send WhatsApps?** It opens the phone's dialer or WhatsApp with everything pre-filled; you tap call or send. Browsers can't dial or send silently, and that confirm tap is a safety feature. A native Android app is next on the roadmap for fully hands-free sending.
- **Does it know everything?** It uses one of the strongest models plus live web search, so it covers every school and college subject. Like every AI, it can make mistakes, so we show sources.
- **How does it know which language to answer in?** Every message goes through a detector (script + common words, 137 languages). Spoken audio is transcribed by Gemini, which also reports the language. You can override it by saying "reply in Tamil" or pin a language with the 🌐 button.
- **Privacy?** Contacts and memory live on your own server. No biometrics are stored, transcripts can be turned off, and you can export or erase everything.
